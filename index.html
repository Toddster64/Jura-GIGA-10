Pro Tips

  • Use only roasted, untreated coffee beans for best results
  • Clean the milk system daily to prevent clogs
  • Descale regularly based on your water hardness level
  • Keep the machine level and in a well-ventilated area
  • Use the dual grinders for different bean types
